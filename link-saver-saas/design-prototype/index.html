Optimize Your Morning Routine for Peak Performance
Stanford neuroscientist specializing in brain optimization and sleep research.
morning routine cortisol sunlight circadian
TLDR
Morning sunlight within 30 minutes of waking triggers a cortisol pulse that sets your entire circadian rhythm for the next 14+ hours.
Key Takeaways
  • Get 5-10 minutes of direct sunlight within 30 minutes of waking — even on cloudy days (10x more lux than indoor lighting)
  • The cortisol pulse from morning light exposure is beneficial and distinct from chronic stress cortisol
  • Caffeine should be delayed 90-120 minutes after waking to avoid afternoon crash
  • Cold exposure (1-3 min cold shower) increases dopamine by 250% for 3+ hours
Actions
Get outdoor sunlight within 30 min of waking
Triggers the cortisol pulse that anchors your circadian clock for 14+ hours, improving sleep onset that night by 30-45 min.
Delay caffeine 90-120 min after waking
Allows natural cortisol to clear adenosine first, preventing the afternoon crash that occurs when caffeine wears off before adenosine is cleared.
